# Threat model for [deployment name]

## Scope
Describe the system and the deployment, the time period you're covering and the outcome you're worried about.

## Assets and actors
Describe what could be lost or damaged, and who or what could act against it (e.g., the model, its users or outsiders).

## Pathways (rank 3 to 5)
For each pathway, cover these points.
- What happens, step by step
- Capability (i.e., could the model do it?)
- Propensity (i.e., would a misaligned model want to, and would it try here?)
- Opportunity (i.e., what in this deployment makes it possible?)
- The controls that already exist and where they fall short

## Evidence that would change my mind
Say what result would make you rank a pathway higher or lower.

## First three tests
For each test, give the question, the method, what a pass and a fail would look like, and the access you'd need.
