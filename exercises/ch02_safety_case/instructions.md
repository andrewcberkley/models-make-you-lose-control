# Chapter 2 exercise. Break down a safety case

Read safety_case.md (fictional), which is the safety case Supa Dupa Labs wrote for WorkIt (i.e., the same lab and agent as the Chapter 1 exercise), and work out how much weight it can really bear.

1. List every claim the case depends on, including the ones it never states.
2. Pick the five weakest links, and for each one say why it's weak, what could go wrong as a result and what evidence would fix it.
3. Say what kind of safety argument this is (i.e., inability, control or trustworthiness) and whether the evidence matches that kind.
4. Finish with a three-sentence bottom line in plain words, covering what the case shows, what it doesn't and what would change your mind.

> **Why the name?** I named Supa Dupa Labs after Missy Elliott's 1997 album *Supa Dupa Fly*, because its safety case sounds supa dupa on a first read, and your job is to find out whether it's really worth it.

Only compare your answer with the solutions to the exercises once you've finished.
